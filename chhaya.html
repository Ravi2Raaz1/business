About Us

Chhayavi is an Art & Innovation-based entity that focuses on creative and artistic expression through projects, workshops, and events. It provides a platform for artists, creators, and art lovers to express and connect.

Our Objectives

  • Create spaces that foster inclusivity, self-exploration, and expression.
  • Encourage traditional and modern art forms through community collaboration.
  • Engage in therapeutic and festive art experiences.
  • Promote sustainable living and waste management through creative workshops.
  • Collaborate with social organizations for meaningful impact.
